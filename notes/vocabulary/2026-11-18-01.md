## 原题｜FIB-RW #20 University of Maryland（2026-11-18）

The University of Maryland boasts 78 academic programs (1) \_\_\_\_ in the top 25 nationally and 29 academic programs in the top 10 according to U.S. News and World report. By drawing top-notch faculty, attracting the brightest students and (2) \_\_\_\_ in the quality of our academic programs, we are a force to (3) \_\_\_\_ with on a national (4) \_\_\_\_.

截图可见选项：ranked / participating / investing / agree / reckon / perspective / basis / ranged。

## 错因与语法

1. \*\*invest in sth.\*\*＝向……投入资金或资源；participate in sth.＝参与……。两者都可接 in，但本题强调投入资源提升 academic programs 的质量，故为 investing。并列结构：By drawing…, attracting… and investing…，by 后均用动名词。
2. **a force to reckon with**＝不容小觑的力量／强有力的竞争者；不能替换成 a force to agree with。也可见 a force to be reckoned with。
3. **on a national basis**＝在全国范围内；**on a ... basis** 为固定框架，例 on a regular / daily / individual basis。**perspective**＝视角、观点，常用 **from my perspective / from a different perspective**，而非 on a national perspective。
