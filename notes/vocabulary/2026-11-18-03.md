## 题目｜FIB-R #44 The long-term goal（2026-11-18）

The long-term goal can be divided into short-term goals that are (1) \_\_\_\_ and achievable. For example, (2) \_\_\_\_ achieve a high grade, you need to set short-term objectives such as studying regularly, completing assignments on time, and actively participating in class. Achieving these short-term goals along the way will help you reach your long-term goal. And, (3) \_\_\_\_ action on feedback also plays an important role in this process.

截图备选词：realistic / in order to / frequent / naturalistic / timely / along the way。

- ① realistic ✅ → realistic（正确）

- ③ frequent ❌ → **timely**（平台正确答案）

## 错因分析

第③空：**timely action on feedback** = 及时根据反馈采取行动；**frequent** = 频繁的，强调次数多；**timely** = 及时的，强调时机恰当。频繁行动并非不合语法，但反馈需要及时处理，timely 与句意更贴切。错误类型：近义词语义和搭配选择。

## 易混淆词

- timely：及时的，重视采取行动的时机。
- frequent：频繁的，重视发生的次数。
- regular：定期的／有规律的，重视规律性。
- naturalistic：自然主义的、写实的，不等于 realistic（现实可行的）。
