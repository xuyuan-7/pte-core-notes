## 2026-11-22｜FIB D&D #30 Perks｜本次练习与复测

- **题型：**FIB D&D（阅读拖拽填空）；截图本次答对 **3/5**。
- **五空平台答案：**scheme、ensure、avoid、includes、treatment。
- **错误空1：**company pension **scheme**（本次选 consideration）。**pension scheme** 是养老金计划；consideration = 考虑、考虑因素，不能代替 scheme。
- **错误空4：**if an employer **includes** an entitlement to low-interest loans in employees' contracts（本次选 allows）。**include A in B** = 把某项内容包含在合同或方案中。**allow somebody to do something** = 允许某人做某事，不能直接代替 include A in B。
- **答对空：**ensure that ...（确保……）；in order to avoid discrimination（为了避免歧视）；unequal treatment（不平等待遇）。
- **原文重点：**Employers often offer employees perks in addition to cash wages ... company pension scheme. They must ensure that they are fair ... in order to avoid discrimination. ... take the employer to court on the basis of unequal treatment.
- **迁移复测：**“The company \_\_\_\_\_\_ free health insurance in its employee benefits package.” 用户选择 **B. includes**，**答对 1 题**；属于一次新语境正确，不覆盖原题 3/5，也不自动判为长期掌握。

## 正确词汇与固定搭配

| 英语表达 | 中文 | 用法和辨析 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| perks | 额外福利、津贴 | Employers offer employees perks. |
| in addition to | 除……以外（还） | in addition to cash wages = 除现金工资以外 |
| pension scheme | 养老金计划 | scheme = 计划/制度；company pension scheme |
| ensure that + 句子 | 确保…… | ensure that they are fair |
| in order to + 动词原形 | 为了…… | in order to avoid discrimination |
| avoid discrimination | 避免歧视 | avoid + 名词 / V-ing |
| include A in B | 把 A 包含在 B 中 | include an entitlement in a contract；主语 an employer 单数，谓语 includes |
| allow somebody to do something | 允许某人做某事 | allow employees to work from home；和 include A in B 区分 |
| an entitlement to something | 享有某事物的权利 | an entitlement to low-interest loans |
| low-interest loans | 低息贷款 | low-interest 为复合形容词 |
| unequal treatment | 不平等待遇 | on the basis of unequal treatment |
| take somebody to court | 起诉某人 | take the employer to court |

- **迁移：**include A in B 已在新句中答对一次；下一步再测试 allow somebody to do something。
- **未提供：**本题是否首次遇见、是否犹豫均未确认，暂不推断。

## 2026-11-22｜FIB #78 Native Species in North America｜本次练习 1/4

- **题型：**FIB 阅读填空；截图结果 **1/4**，三个错空，一个正确空；首次/复练未确认。
- **第1空：**“Of the more than 1,000 bat species worldwide, 22 are **native** to North America.” 本次选 local，平台答案 native。**be native to** = 原产于；local 仅指当地的，不等于原生于该地。
- **第2空：**“gardeners should **champion** those that do live here, because they're insectivorous.” 本次选 breed，平台答案 champion。**champion（动词）** = 支持、拥护、倡导；breed = 繁育。上下文强调蝙蝠帮助捕食昆虫，应支持和保护。
- **第3空：**“These bats **consume** moths, beetles and mosquitoes.” 本次选 consume，正确。consume = 食用、消耗；insectivorous = 食虫的。
- **第4空：**“They also protect gardens and crops from such **pests** as cucumber beetles, cutworms and leafhoppers.” 本次选 animals，平台答案 pests。**pests** = 害虫、有害生物；animals = 动物，不能突出危害农作物的语境。
- **理解：**文章说明北美蝙蝠吃飞蛾、甲虫、蚊子，能够帮助花园和农作物抵御害虫。
- **复测：**已给出 champion 迁移题，但用户尚未提交作答；不计为通过。

## FIB #78｜正确词汇与固定搭配

| 英语表达 | 中文 | 用法和辨析 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| be native to | 原产于、原生于 | 22 are native to North America；local 不等于 native |
| native species | 本土物种 | 动植物原生区域的描述 |
| champion（动词） | 支持、拥护、积极倡导 | champion the protection of endangered species；不同于 breed = 繁殖 |
| insectivorous | 食虫的 | 表示以昆虫为食，帮助判断 consume |
| consume insects | 食用昆虫 | consume moths, beetles and mosquitoes |
| pests | 害虫、有害生物 | 作物保护语境比 animals 更精准 |
| protect A from B | 保护 A 免受 B 侵害 | protect gardens and crops from pests |
| such ... as ... | 例如……、诸如…… | such pests as cucumber beetles |

- **已答对：**consume 本次正确；不推断所有搭配已长期掌握。
- **下一步：**一题一题做新语境迁移，先 champion，再 native to 和 pests；后续复测不覆盖本次原题成绩。
