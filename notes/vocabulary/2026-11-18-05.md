## 原题｜FIB-R #33 Educational Programs（2026-11-18）

We (1) \_\_\_\_ anyone can excel with the right foundation. By (2) \_\_\_\_ the basis of knowledge, such as coding and other skills, participants gain essential expertise. Through 9–12 projects, you will be introduced to (3) \_\_\_\_ your portfolio, and when you (4) \_\_\_\_ the program, you will have a robust showcase of your abilities.

截图可见备选词：believe / building / understanding / complete / think / work / solving。

## 实际作答与正确答案

- 本次平台成绩：1/4（25%）；未确认是否首次作答。
- ① believe → believe ✅
- ② building → understanding ❌。By + V-ing 两项在形式上都可成立，关键是上下文含义：understand the basics＝理解基础知识；build a foundation＝打基础。原题 the basis of knowledge 略不自然，忠实保留。
- ③ complete → building ❌。be introduced to + 名词/动名词，此处 to 是介词；building your portfolio＝逐步建立作品集。complete 为原形，不能直接接在此处介词后。
- ④ understanding → complete ❌。when you + 谓语动词；时间状语从句表示将来时用一般现在时：When you complete the program, you will have...；understanding 不能单独作谓语。

## 重点知识与固定搭配

| 英文 | 中文 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| understand the basics | 理解基础知识 | 理解 vs 建立 |
| build a foundation | 打下基础 | build 与 foundation 常搭配 |
| by doing sth. | 通过做某事 | by 是介词 |
| gain expertise | 获得专业能力 | expertise 不可数 |
| be introduced to doing sth. | 初步接触／学习做某事 | to 为介词 |
| build a portfolio | 建立作品集 | 常见搭配 |
| complete a program | 完成课程／项目 | complete 作谓语 |
| a showcase of abilities | 能力展示 | showcase 名词 |
