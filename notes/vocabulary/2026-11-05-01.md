## FIB-RW #37｜Story Titles｜4/5

错误：through allow → **through allowing**。through 为介词，后接动词时使用 V-ing；不是进行时。

原句：Novices are peculiarly liable to this fault, usually through allowing themselves to be too easily satisfied.

## 固定搭配

| English | 中文与例句 |
| --- | --- |
| through allowing | 通过允许／由于让自己……；They improved through practicing. |
| be liable to sth. | 容易……；Novices are liable to this fault. |
| allow oneself to do sth. | 允许自己做某事；Do not allow yourself to give up. |
| go to infinite pains to do sth. | 不遗余力做某事；She went to infinite pains to prepare. |
| a commonplace phrase | 老套的短语；Avoid a commonplace phrase. |
| satisfy one's curiosity | 满足某人的好奇心；The ending satisfied my curiosity. |

## 生词

| English | 中文、音标及例句 |
| --- | --- |
| novice /ˈnɒvɪs/ | 新手；He is a novice writer. |
| peculiarly /pɪˈkjuːliəli/ | 尤其；特别；She was peculiarly quiet. |
| commonplace /ˈkɒmənpleɪs/ | 平凡的；陈腐的；This is a commonplace idea. |
| lest /lest/ | 以免；She whispered lest anyone hear. |
| denouement /deɪˈnuːmɒ̃/ | （故事）结局；The denouement was surprising. |

复习重点：介词 through/by/without/after/before + V-ing；本题仅第二空错误，其余 liable、infinite、commonplace、satisfy 正确。

## FIB-RW #47｜Changes for New B.C. Residents｜3/4（🟠）

错误：because of → 题库答案 due to。两者均可接名词短语表示原因，此处 because of 在现代英语中也可成立，不要误记成语法错误。

原句：However, their premiums will be adjusted for the first three years of driving in B.C. due to the increased risks associated with driving in a new area.

### 固定搭配

| English | 中文与例句 |
| --- | --- |
| due to + noun | 由于；The delay was due to rain. |
| because of + noun | 因为；The flight was delayed because of rain. |
| because + clause | 因为（接完整句子）；We stayed home because it rained. |
| be associated with | 与……有关；Risks are associated with driving. |
| be required to do sth. | 被要求做某事；You are required to provide proof. |
| upon receipt of | 收到……之后；We will reply upon receipt of your letter. |
| credit sb. with sth. | 认可、计入某人的资历；They credited her with ten years of experience. |

### 生词

| English | 中文、音标、例句 |
| --- | --- |
| premium /ˈpriːmiəm/ | 保险费；Insurance premiums may increase. |
| abstract /ˈæbstrækt/ | 摘要；driver's abstract＝驾驶记录证明；Bring your driver's abstract. |
| crash-free /ˈkræʃ friː/ | 无事故的；She remained crash-free. |

复习重点：🟠 近义表达辨析；because + 从句；because of / due to + 名词短语。

## FIB-R #9｜National Portrait Gallery｜2/5（🔴）

错题：① expertise → core；④ core → consists；⑤ contains → expertise。② accessible、③ dates 正确。

原文重点：The Conservation Department performs one of the Gallery's core functions ... to make them accessible now and in future. The Collection dates from the 8th century to the present day, and consists of portraits in a variety of media, so the Gallery employs Conservators with expertise in a range of disciplines.

### 固定搭配

| English | 中文、例句 |
| --- | --- |
| core functions | 核心职能；Preservation is one of the gallery's core functions. |
| make sth. accessible | 使某物可获得／可使用；Libraries make knowledge accessible. |
| date from | 始于、追溯到；The building dates from the 18th century. |
| consist of | 由……组成；The team consists of five members. |
| expertise in | 在……方面有专长；She has expertise in software development. |
| a variety of | 各种各样的；They use a variety of materials. |
| a range of disciplines | 多个专业领域；The team works across a range of disciplines. |

### 生词

| English | 中文、音标、例句 |
| --- | --- |
| core /kɔːr/ | 核心（的）；Safety is a core value. |
| expertise /ˌekspɜːrˈtiːz/ | 专业知识、专长；He has expertise in design. |
| accessible /əkˈsesəbl/ | 可获得的、可使用的；The information is accessible online. |
| conservator /kənˈsɜːrvətər/ | 文物保护修复人员；A conservator restored the painting. |
| preservation /ˌprezərˈveɪʃən/ | 保存、保护；Preservation of old paintings is important. |
| discipline /ˈdɪsəplɪn/ | 学科、专业领域；Biology is a scientific discipline. |

### 语法与错因

① core 是形容词，修饰 functions；expertise 是名词，不能直接作此处的形容词。④ The Collection dates ... and consists ...：and 并列两个谓语动词，consists of 为固定搭配。⑤ with 后需要名词 expertise，构成 with expertise in。复习优先级：🔴 高；重点复习词性、并列谓语与固定搭配。

## FIB-R #11｜Teacher Recruitment｜3/5（🔴）

- 第一空：误选 **strength**，正确 **effective**。原句：word of mouth is by far their most **effective** recruitment tool。most + 形容词 effective（有效的）；strength /streŋθ/ 是名词（力量、优势），不能这样直接修饰 recruitment tool。
- 第五空：误选 **boom**，正确 **boon**。原句：News coverage has proved to be a **boon to** alternative ...。用户明确表示 **boon 是不认识的生词**，不只是拼写看错；列入重点生词、下次复习优先抽查。
- 其余三空正确：spread、effort、especially。

### 生词（🔴 重点）

| 英文 | 音标 | 中文与例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **boon** | /buːn/ | n. 益处、好处；Online learning is a boon to remote students.（在线学习对偏远地区学生大有裨益。）**本次首次不认识** |
| boom | /buːm/ | n. 繁荣、激增；There was a boom in tourism.（旅游业出现繁荣。） |
| effective | /ɪˈfektɪv/ | adj. 有效的；This is an effective method. |
| strength | /streŋθ/ | n. 力量、优势；Teamwork is our strength. |

### 固定搭配与辨析

- \*\*a boon to sb./sth.\*\*＝对……有益的事物；注意 **boon to**。
- \*\*a boom in sth.\*\*＝某领域的繁荣/激增；注意 **boom in**。两词仅差 n/m，但语义不同。
- **word of mouth**＝口口相传；**spread the word**＝传播消息。
- **effective recruitment tool**＝有效的招聘工具；**without any special effort**＝不费特别的力气。

**复习重点**：先独立回忆 boon /buːn/ 的中文含义和 a boon to，再对比 a boom in；复习形容词 effective 与名词 strength 的词性区别。标红，纳入本周FIB词汇总结及JSON复习清单。

## FIB-R #12 Music（2/3）

第二空选 classes，正确为 examples。原文关键表达：name a few canonical examples。这里指列举经典音乐作品的具体例子，不是音乐类别。

重点生词：canonical /kəˈnɒnɪkəl/，形容词，经典的、公认的、权威的。用户明确说明不认识 canonical，标记为红色重点生词。

固定搭配：canonical examples（经典例子）；canonical works（经典作品）；knowledge of music（对音乐的了解）。

辨析：examples 是具体例子，classes 是类别。复习时优先抽查 canonical 的词义及 name a few canonical examples 的语境。

## FIB-L #28｜Growth of Technology Systems｜1/3（🔴）｜2026-11-05

- 第一空：未填写；正确 **burgeon**。原句：The tech industry continues to burgeon as new innovations emerge every year.
- 第二空：systems，正确。
- 第三空：误填 burgeon；正确 **contribution**。原句：Their contribution to society is significant, making processes faster and more accurate.

### 生词

| English | 中文、音标及例句 |
| --- | --- |
| burgeon /ˈbɜːrdʒən/ | v. 迅速增长、蓬勃发展；The tech industry continues to burgeon. |
| contribution /ˌkɒntrɪˈbjuːʃən/ | n. 贡献；Their contribution to society is significant. |
| significant /sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/ | adj. 显著的、重大的；Technology has a significant impact. |

### 固定搭配与语法

- **continue to + 动词原形**：continues to burgeon（持续蓬勃发展）。
- **contribution to + 名词**：contribution to society（对社会的贡献）。
- **a significant contribution to ...**：对……的重要贡献。
- **their + 名词**：Their contribution ... is significant；burgeon 是动词，不能直接填在这里充当名词。

**复习重点（🔴）**：听辨 burgeon 和 contribution；结合词性和上下文判断空格；第二空 systems 已正确。首次作答1/3，不将纠错后的熟悉程度当作首次正确。
