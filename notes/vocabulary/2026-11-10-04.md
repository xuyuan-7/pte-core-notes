## FIB-RW #52｜Company Role｜2026-11-10

截图可见5空：**4/5 正确**。用户的答案依次为 comfortable ✅，contribution ❌（平台正确 position），while ✅，forward ✅，know ✅。

### 原题上下文（按截图）

If you feel **comfortable**, give some information about company activities. I would appreciate any information reference about that job. I would like to know more about the role and understand the requirements to make a **position** within your company. I gained experience **while** I worked in the ABC company, and I am eager to explore new opportunities to apply my skills in a challenging environment. I look **forward** to hearing from you. Let me **know** if there is any further information you require.

### 易错词

- **position**（名词）职位、岗位、位置。a position within your company＝贵公司的一个职位；**apply for a position**＝申请职位；**hold a position**＝担任职位。
- **contribution**（名词）贡献；**make a contribution to**＝对……作出贡献。截图原文 *make a position within your company* 本身不够自然，通常会说 *obtain/secure a position within your company*；*make a contribution to your company* 才是典型固定搭配。需避免把原题不自然的表达当成英语标准搭配。
- **feel comfortable**＝觉得自在、舒适；**while + 从句**＝当……时；\*\*look forward to doing sth.\*\*＝期待做某事；**let sb. know**＝让某人知道。

### 状态

截图显示平台判定第二空贡献→职位；此处忠实保留平台标准答案，也提示原文表达不自然。用户选 contribution 的理由尚未确认，先通过对比 position 与 contribution 的语义与搭配诊断，再将错因定为确证。

### 用户真实选词思路确认（2026-11-10）

用户选择 **C**：认识 position 和 contribution 两个词，但根据整句意思判断 contribution 更合适，并非因为不认识 position。原句 <code>to make a ___ within your company</code> 本身搭配不自然；<code>make a contribution to your company</code> 才是惯用搭配，<code>obtain/secure a position within your company</code> 才是惯用表达。平台判定 position 正确，用户作答 contribution 错误（保留4/5成绩）；但此处不能归因为词义不熟，应标记为「上下文语义合理推断与平台指定答案冲突／原题措辞有问题」。复习建议重点理解介词 to/within 和动词 make/secure 的搭配区别，不需要死记 <code>make a position</code>。
