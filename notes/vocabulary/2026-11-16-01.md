### #126 Impressionist Painters｜2/4

<details>

<details>
<summary>答案与错因<br />1 radical（误选 creatures）；2 generations（正确）；3 subjects（正确）；4 source（误选 outcome）。</summary>

- earlier generations＝前几代人；subjects＝绘画题材。
- 复习：辨别 source 与 outcome 的因果方向。

### #141 Plagiarism｜3/5

<details>
<summary>原题（先填空）<br />How is plagiarism detected? It is usually easy for lecturers to (1) \_\_\_\_\_ plagiarism within students' work. The University also actively investigates plagiarism in students' assessed work through electronic detection software called Turnitin. This software (2) \_\_\_\_\_ students' work (3) \_\_\_\_\_ text on the Internet, in journal articles and within previously (4) \_\_\_\_\_ work (from LSBU and other institutions) and highlights any matches it (5) \_\_\_\_\_.</summary>


<details>
<summary>答案与错因<br />1 identify（正确）；2 compares（正确）；3 against（误选 to）；4 submitted（正确）；5 finds（误选 committed）。</summary>


- compare A against B＝将A与B对照核查；compare A to B 本身也成立，原句查重核对语境以 against 更贴切，不要把 to 当作普遍语法错误。
- any matches (that) it finds：省略关系代词 that 的定语从句；it 是主语，finds 是谓语。committed（实施、投入的过去式/分词）不合句意。
- identify plagiarism＝识别抄袭；previously submitted work＝此前提交的作业。
- 复习：compare against / compare with / compare to；关系代词省略的定语从句。

</details>

### #145 Climate｜4/5｜用时00:56

<details>
<summary>原题（先填空）<br />Climate is the word we (1) \_\_\_\_\_ for weather over a long period of time. The desert has a (2) \_\_\_\_\_ climate, because there (3) \_\_\_\_\_ very little rain. The UK (4) \_\_\_\_\_ a temperate climate, which means winters are, overall, mild (5) \_\_\_\_\_ summers, generally, don't get too hot.</summary>


<details>
<summary>答案与错因<br />1 use（正确）；2 dry（正确）；3 is（误选 are）；4 has（正确）；5 and（正确）。</summary>


- 重点错误：there is very little rain。rain 表示降雨/雨水时为不可数名词，there be 依后方名词主谓一致，因此用 is，不用 are。
- little + 不可数名词（little rain / little information）；few + 可数复数（few students）；a little / a few 表示有一点／几个。
- 正确搭配：use A for B＝用A表示B；dry climate＝干燥气候；temperate climate＝温带/温和气候；mild winters＝温和的冬季。
- 复习建议：练习 there is / there are 与可数、不可数名词搭配；本题做对的四空不视为错题。

</details>

### #247 Recruitment｜复做2/3（首次0/3）

<details>
<summary>原题（先填空）<br />Finding challenging or (1) \_\_\_\_\_ employment may mean retraining and moving from a stale or boring job in order to find your (2) \_\_\_\_\_ and pursue it. The idea is to think long range and anticipate an active lifestyle into later years -- perhaps into one's 80s or 90s. Being personally productive may now mean anticipating retiring in stages. This might indicate going to an alternate (3) \_\_\_\_\_ should a current career end by choice or economic change.</summary>


<details>
<summary>答案、作答与错因<br />正确答案：1 rewarding；2 passion；3 plan。首次练习三个空均错误（具体首次错误选项未提供）；本次：1 direction → rewarding（错），2 passion（对），3 plan（对）。</summary>


- challenging or rewarding employment：challenging 与 rewarding 都是形容词，并列修饰 employment；direction 是名词，词性不匹配。
- rewarding＝有回报的、有成就感的；rewarding career / rewarding experience / rewarding employment。
- find your passion and pursue it＝找到自己的热爱并追求它；pursue 是及物动词，直接接宾语（pursue your passion / pursue a career）。
- an alternate plan＝替代方案／备选计划；alternate 是形容词，后接名词；backup plan＝备用计划。
- stale or boring job＝陈旧乏味的工作；retraining＝重新培训；anticipate＝预期；retire in stages＝分阶段退休。
- should a current career end＝if a current career should end，条件倒装，表示“如果目前职业结束”。
- 复习建议：保持红色；优先练习 and/or 并列词性、rewarding 的词性和搭配、pursue 的及物动词用法及 alternate plan；两空虽在复做正确，尚未确认迁移掌握。

</details>

### #247 追问补充｜为什么不是 direction？

- **find your direction**：找到自己的方向，语法成立；但原文是“find your \_\_\_ and pursue it”，**pursue your passion**（追求热爱）是更自然的搭配，且前文 stale or boring job 与 rewarding employment 强调找回职业热情。
- **find your passion and pursue it**：找到自己真正热爱的事情并追求它；it 指代 passion。
- **an alternate direction**：另一个方向，英语中可以成立；原文讨论退休规划，以及“should a current career end”（假如当前职业结束），**an alternate plan**（备选方案）更贴合应对职业变化的规划语境。
- **关键辨析**：direction＝方向；passion＝热爱；plan＝计划/方案。三者不能单凭中文都说得通来互换。解题时先看词性，再看动词搭配，最后结合上下文选最恰当的表达。
- **复习关注**：类似“语法可行但搭配自然度与篇章语义不同”的选项；对 direction 不要错误地记为永远不可用。

### FIB-RW #52 Company Role｜when 与 while 辨析（本次 5 空全部正确）

<details>
<summary>原题（截图所示）<br />If you feel (1) \_\_\_\_\_, give some information about company activities. I would appreciate any information reference about that job. I would like to know more about the role and understand the requirements to make a (2) \_\_\_\_\_ within your company. I gained experience (3) \_\_\_\_\_ I worked in the ABC company, and I am eager to explore new opportunities to apply my skills in a challenging environment. I look (4) \_\_\_\_\_ to hearing from you. Let me (5) \_\_\_\_\_ if there is any further information you require.</summary>


<details>
<summary>正确答案及本次作答<br />1 comfortable；2 position；3 while；4 forward；5 know。截图五个空均打勾；页面顶部另显示 14/15，不能直接将其等同于本题 5 空的正确数。</summary>


核心提问：为什么是 while，而不是 when？

- while I worked in the ABC company：强调在公司工作的持续期间积累经验；while 后不一定要接进行时，简单过去时也可以。
- when I worked in the ABC company：语法也成立，侧重“当时/那时候”；原句表达工作期间逐渐积累经验，while 更符合强调过程的语境。不要把 when 误记为语法错误。
- when I arrived home：arrived 表示一个时间点，较自然使用 when；while I was studying：强调一段持续过程。
- gain experience＝积累经验；look forward to hearing from you＝期待收到回复，to 是介词，后接 -ing；let me know＝请告知。
- 复习：对比 I gained experience while I worked there / I gained experience when I worked there；根据语义而非单看时态选 while/when。

</details>

</details>
