## FIB-RW #33｜Distance Learning｜最后一空（🟠 做对但不确定）

**原题句子：** Since there are no on-campus courses to attend, students can learn from their own homes, at work on their lunch breaks and from virtually anywhere with internet access.

**正确答案：** Since（因为、由于）。

**答题情况：** 实际选择 Since，答对；当时纠结 Besides。非错题，属于正确但不确定。

| 英文／搭配 | 中文释义与辨析 |
| --- | --- |
| Since + 完整句子 | 因为／由于，连接原因从句与结果主句。 |
| Besides, + 完整句子 | 此外／而且，补充信息；属于连接副词，不能像 Since 一样直接将两个独立分句连接起来。 |

**语法与语境：** 不需要去校园上课（原因）→ 学生能从家里、工作场所等地点学习（结果）。Since 引出原因从句；Besides 表示补充，不构成这里所需的因果连接。在原句结构中直接替换成 Besides 还会造成逗号拼接问题。

**相关易混淆用法：** Because / Since + 从句；because of / due to + 名词（短语）；Besides, / In addition, 用于补充论点。

**复习建议：** 先看空格后是从句还是名词短语，再判断上下文是因果、转折还是递进。下次不依赖答案能判断再标绿。

## FIB-RW #35｜Exercise and …｜第三空（🔴 选错）

**原题：** However, it's important to talk to your doctor before starting an exercise \_\_\_\_\_\_. He or she might have advice on what exercises are safe and any precautions you might need to take while exercising.

**本次答案：** operation（错误）；**正确答案：** routine。

**错误原因：** 把 operation 与表示固定日常安排的 routine 混淆；用户自己指出可能记得其他带 operation 的搭配，但尚未确认究竟是哪一个，不把猜测记为已确认原因。

| 英文／固定搭配 | 中文及区别 |
| --- | --- |
| exercise routine | 日常锻炼计划；规律、反复进行的活动安排。 |
| daily routine | 日常惯例、固定安排。 |
| surgical operation / perform an operation | 外科手术／进行手术。 |
| business operations | 经营、运营活动。 |
| operation of a machine / machine operation | 机器的操作、运行。 |
| standard operating procedure | 标准操作流程；procedure 偏按顺序执行的步骤。 |
| take precautions | 采取预防措施。 |
| chronic disease | 慢性疾病。 |
| health benefits | 健康益处。 |

**关键辨析：** routine 是重复进行的固定习惯或安排；operation 常指手术、操作、运营；procedure 指程序、操作步骤。这些词有交集，但 exercise routine 才是本题最自然的搭配。

**相关已见题：** Robotic Surgery 原题里的 real operations 指真实手术，operate remotely 指远程进行手术。这是可能的联想来源，不代表用户已确认。

**复习建议：** 🔴 保留红色直到能独立辨认 exercise routine / surgical operation / standard operating procedure，并在新语境完成迁移。其他三空 chronic / benefits / advice 正确，不误记为错题。

## FIB-R #45｜invite parents｜第五空（🟠 做对，但词形需要巩固）

**原题：** We invite parents to participate in a program held three times a year. The details of the program will be posted on our website. We expect parents to attend so that their children can **succeed**.

**实际作答：** held / posted / expect / attend / succeed，**5/5全对**。用户追问“为什么是successed”，表示对 succeed 与 success、succeeded 的词性和形式仍不牢固；**不记为做错**，暂按🟠待巩固。

| 英文／语法 | 中文释义及辨析 |
| --- | --- |
| succeed（动词） | 成功；情态动词 can 后接原形：can succeed。 |
| success（名词） | 成功；achieve success。 |
| successful（形容词） | 成功的；be successful。 |
| succeeded（动词过去式/过去分词） | 已经成功了；不能直接放在 can 后：can succeeded 不正确。 |
| so that + 主语 + can + 动词原形 | 以便……能够……；so that their children can succeed。 |
| expect sb. to do sth. | 期望某人做某事；expect parents to attend。 |
| be held + 时间频率 | 被举办；a program held three times a year。 |
| be posted on a website | 被发布在网站上；will be posted on our website。 |

**核心规则：** can / could / may / might / must / should / will / would + 动词原形。动词 succeed 不加 -ed；“successed”不是 succeed 的正确过去式。

**复习建议：** 用新语境区别 can succeed / achieve success / be successful，检查情态动词后原形。不要把这道5/5题误标成错题或直接标绿。

## 2026-11-14｜FIB-RW #49｜Job Interview｜confidence / confident（🔴 实际选错）

**截图原句：** You are very happy to be considered by the company for a position. You have **confidence** that you can leave a good impression on the company. In the past, you were responsible for certain tasks in your previous job as an XXX. Finally, you got the opportunity to have an in-person interview.

**实际结果：** 5空中4空正确，第二空填了 **confident**，正确答案 **confidence**；其余四空均正确。因本空词性混淆，标记为🔴红色。迁移题“is very \_\_\_\_\_\_ that ...”尚未作答，不推断已掌握。

| 词汇或搭配 | 中文解释及正确用法 |
| --- | --- |
| confidence（名词） | 信心：have confidence；have confidence that + 从句。 |
| confident（形容词） | 自信的：be confident；be confident that + 从句。 |
| confidently（副词） | 自信地：speak confidently；answer confidently。 |
| leave a good impression on sb. | 给某人留下好印象。 |
| be responsible for sth. / doing sth. | 负责某事／做某事。 |
| have/get the opportunity to do sth. | 有机会做某事。 |
| be considered for a position | 被考虑录用某个职位。 |

**做题方法：** 看空格前的词和句法位置：have 后本句需要名词 confidence；be + confident + that 从句则使用形容词。先判词性，再判断含义。

**复习建议：** 下次用新句区分 have confidence / be confident / speak confidently，并复习 impression on、responsible for、opportunity to。此题不误记其余四空为错题。

## 2026-11-14｜FIB-RW #67｜Letter to School Officials｜hope / expect（🔴）

**截图原文（填入正确答案）：** A letter from a mother to school officials. Because her son was **sick** at home she explained his illness to them saying she did not want her son to **spread** the disease to other children. She **hoped** that the authorities would be able to **pass** on lessons to her son during this **period**.

**结果：** 5空中4空正确；第三空用户选 **expected**，题库正确答案 **hoped**。其余四空均正确。🔴 红色；尚未完成迁移题，不标记为掌握。

**正确用法与语义：** **hope that + 从句** 表达对某事发生的愿望；**expect that + 从句** 表达预期、认为某事可能或应当发生；**expect sb. to do sth.** 表达期待某人做某事。该空母亲希望学校继续向孩子提供课程，hoped 更贴合请求语境；但 expected that ... 本身语法成立，不能将其当作错误结构。

**正确搭配：** **be sick at home**（生病在家）；**spread a disease**（传播疾病）；**pass on sth. to sb.**（把某事物传递给某人）；**during this period**（在这段期间）。

**复习重点：** 在新语境中区分说话人的希望与预期；保留语法正确、语义侧重点不同的说明。只记录正确表达和规范例句，不将正确的 expected 结构当成错误英语。

## 2026-11-14｜FIB-RW #69｜Study Environment｜助动词省略（🔴 原题错，迁移题部分正确）

**原题截图（正确填空）：** Some students say that they need complete quiet to read and study. Others study best in a crowded, noisy room because the noise actually **helps** them concentrate. Some students like quiet music playing; others **do** not. The point is, you should know the level of noise that is optimal for your own studying. However, one general rule for all students is that the television seems to be more of a distraction than music or other background noise, so **leave** the TV off when you are reading or studying. **Also**, don't let yourself become distracted by computer games, email, or Internet surfing.

**原题结果：** 4/5；第二空用户选 **are**，正确为 **do**；其他四空正确。🔴 原题尚未在新一轮独立复习中验证。

**语法教学（只作为正确用法记忆）：** ① Some students **like** quiet music playing; others **do not**. ② Some people **enjoy** working from home; others **do not**. ③ Some students **are** comfortable studying in noisy environments; others **are not**. ④ Some students **can** swim; others **cannot**. 第二分句沿用前一分句动词/助动词类型，省略重复内容；实义动词的否定用 do/does/did not，be 动词用相应 be not，情态动词用相应情态动词 + not。

**额外正确搭配：** **help sb. (to) do sth.**（帮助某人做某事）；**be a distraction**（分心因素）；**leave the TV off**（让电视保持关闭）；**be distracted by sth.**（被……分散注意力）。

**迁移练习状态：** ‘Some people enjoy working from home, while others \_\_\_ not.’ 用户选 B do，正确；‘Some students are comfortable studying in noisy environments, while others \_\_\_ not.’ 用户选 C are，正确。综合双空题尚未回答，不记为完成。下次检查混合动词句型后再考虑由红转橙。
