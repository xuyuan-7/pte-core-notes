## 2026-11-12｜FIB-RW #20 Online and Distance Learning

### 完整原题（截图核对）

If you are interested in studying but have other **commitments**, our online and distance learning courses offer an alternative flexible **mode** of study. Teaching is conducted through a mixture of audio-visual, written and interactive materials. There are also **opportunities** for online discussion and collaborative projects with tutors and other students. Some programs include residential **modules** taught on campus, giving you the chance to meet and work alongside your tutors and fellow students face-to-face. Our Graduate School provides comprehensive support for distance and part-time researchers, many of whom study for up to six years or more.

### 重点词汇与搭配

| 英文 | 中文和语境 |
| --- | --- |
| module (education) | 课程单元、教学模块；不是这里的「软件模块」 |
| residential module | 到校集中学习的课程模块，安排可能涉及短期住宿 |
| taught on campus | 在校园授课；过去分词后置定语 = that are taught on campus |
| complete six modules | 完成六个课程单元 |
| mode of study | 学习方式 |
| commitments | 其他事务、责任、必须履行的安排 |
| opportunities for | ……的机会 |
| work alongside | 与……一起工作、合作 |
| face-to-face | 面对面 |
| part-time researchers | 非全日制研究人员 |

### 不确定知识点与复习

原句 Some programs include residential modules taught on campus. 文中对比线上远程学习与线下校园授课，modules 指教育意义的教学单元，而不是计算机软件模块。teach a module → modules taught on campus（被教授的课程单元）。下一轮用不同教育语境检查词义及过去分词修饰关系，不照搬原题结构；做对但不确定仍保留橙色。
