## FIB-RW #10｜Children and Music｜2026-11-12

- **作答状态：6/6 全对；第5、6空虽答对但不完全确定；建议标橙色🟠。** 本次是否为首次作答未知，不计作首次正确率。
- **六空正确答案：** surrounded / array / limitless / passive / conducted / is intruding。
- **原文（已核对截图）：** The general perception is that children are surrounded by a variety of musical experiences. There are often fewer and fewer opportunities for children to actively engage in music making themselves. They are inundated with music emitting from a wide array of electronic devices, toys, and computers offering a limitless number of musical selections. However, much of the music in children's lives is 'unchosen', in other words they are passive recipients in much of the music in their lives, and not actively engaged in its selection. Studies are being conducted on the effects of the ubiquitous pre-recorded music they encounter and whether or not it is intruding on their desire to make their own music or interact with each other on the playground.

## 不确定点与语法辨析

- **第5空 conducted：** 原句 Studies are being conducted on ...。主语 Studies 承受 conduct 动作，故用 are being conducted；abandoned 语法可能成立但意思是「被放弃」，与研究影响的语境不符。
- **第6空 is intruding：** 原句 whether or not it is intruding on their desire ...。it 指前文的预录制音乐，文章在研究目前持续存在的干扰；is intruding 比 has intruded（已经干扰过）、would have intruded（过去假设结果）、can have intruded（推测过去）更贴合。
