## FIB-R（D&D）#1159 Pandemic｜本次 2/5（40%）

原文（按平台答案还原）：The pandemic created an urgent situation, prompting unprecedented contribution to bolster the health system, reform the pharmaceutical sector, and distribute relief throughout the country.

中文：疫情造成了紧急局面，促使各方作出前所未有的贡献，以加强医疗卫生体系、改革制药行业，并在全国范围内分发救援物资。

- ① urgent（正确）：an urgent situation＝紧急局面。
- ② manufacture → contribution（错误）：unprecedented contribution＝前所未有的贡献；manufacture＝制造。后面三个并列目标指向广泛的投入和贡献。平台原文单数 contribution 略显生硬，自然表达常用 unprecedented contributions 或 unprecedented efforts。
- ③ cost → system（错误）：bolster the health system＝加强医疗体系；cost 指费用，语义不符。reduce health care costs＝降低医疗费用。
- ④ system → sector（错误）：reform the pharmaceutical sector＝改革制药行业。system 强调组织运作的体系，sector 强调行业或领域。
- ⑤ throughout（正确）：throughout the country＝遍及全国。

错因归类：词义、固定搭配、上下文逻辑。截图未显示完整选项；首次作答与否、是否犹豫未知。整题待巩固，三个错误知识点尚未通过复测。

## FIB-R（D&D）#1172 古代绘画（主题概括）｜本次 2/4（50%）

原文（仅截图可见部分，按平台正确答案还原；首尾被截断）：

... created on cave walls or pottery and have survived for thousands of years due to their preservation. The subjects of ancient paintings often include animals, humans, and scenes from daily life. These artworks were not only meant for decoration but also had religious significance. For instance, some cave paintings are believed to have been created for ritualistic purposes or to convey stories and folklore. The use of natural pigments and traditional techniques highlights the resourcefulness and creativity of ancient artists. Despite the passage of time, the beauty and intricacy of ...

- ① preservation（正确）：due to their preservation＝由于得到保存。
- ② folklore → religious（错误）：religious significance＝宗教意义。religious 是形容词，folklore 是名词；后文 ritualistic purposes 提供语义线索。
- ③ mysteries → folklore（错误）：convey stories and folklore＝传达故事和民间传说。mysteries＝谜团；两者都是名词，语法不足以排除，须结合古代绘画传播传统故事的语境选择平台答案。
- ④ traditional（正确）：traditional techniques＝传统技法。

## FIB-R（D&D）#1211 音乐教育（主题概括）｜本次 2/3（66.7%）

原文（截图可见部分，按平台答案还原；首尾不完整）：

... always been an indispensable part of children's education. Today, in most countries around the world, music courses are still offered in schools as part of the basic curriculum. The ancient Greeks believed that music should be performed alongside mathematics and physics, as these subjects complement and enhance one another.

Researchers at The Ohio State University conducted a retrospective study on the behavior of children (mainly high school students) who had been exposed to music courses in their early years or throughout their school life. ...

- ① offered（正确）：music courses are offered＝开设音乐课程。
- ② attached → performed（错误）：perform music＝演奏音乐；attach A to B＝将 A 附加到 B。两者均为过去分词，语法上都可接在 should be 后，须结合语义与搭配。平台原句 performed alongside mathematics and physics 略显生硬；如果强调并列教授学科，taught alongside 更自然，但不是本题已知平台答案。
- ③ exposed（正确）：be exposed to＝接触到；had been exposed to music courses＝此前曾接触音乐课程。

语法：情态动词 + be + 过去分词构成被动语态（should be performed / must be completed / can be used / may be affected）；had been + 过去分词构成过去完成时被动语态。错因主要为词义与搭配辨析，而非单纯语态选择。首次作答与否、犹豫情况及完整选项未知。

## FIB-R（D&D）#1215 Building Sign Systems｜本次 1/3（33.3%）｜用时 01:59

原文（按截图平台答案还原）：Building sign systems bring substantial benefits to building management and assist people in finding their routes. Compared with written signs, graphical signs deliver more intuitive information. Furthermore, lighting conditions also exert an influence on people when they navigate their surroundings.

中文：建筑标识系统为建筑管理带来显著的好处，并帮助人们找到行进路线。与文字标识相比，图形标识能传达更直观的信息。此外，人们在周围环境中辨认方向时，照明条件也会对他们产生影响。

- ① routes → systems（错误）：building sign systems＝建筑标识系统；一套相互配合的指示标识，不是行进路线。system 在今天 #1159 原题也曾选错，此次需继续巩固，不能因短期复测正确就判定掌握。
- ② roads → routes（错误）：find their routes＝找到行进路线；road 是实际道路，route 是从起点到目的地所经过的路径，way 含义较广（find one's way 是自然常用表达；平台答案为 routes，不能仅因复数断定 ways 不合语法）。
- ③ written（正确）：written signs＝文字标识，与 graphical signs 对比。
- 截图其余可见未选选项：systems、ways、tracts、golden；结合已选三项 routes、roads、written。
- 错因：名词词义、固定搭配、建筑内部导航语境。首次作答与否及犹豫情况未知。
