## FIB-R #39｜Advantages of Robotic Surgery（2026-11-19）

- **平台本次成绩：1/3**；是否首次作答：未确认；复测：未完成；掌握状态：🔴错两空，待复测。
- **完整正确原文（依用户截图）**：Robotic surgery allows the entire procedure to be recorded, helping to **preserve** detailed surgical information. This creates a valuable tool for **training**, giving students and professionals the chance to learn from real operations. It also enables doctors to **operate** remotely, a technique known as telesurgery.
- **真实作答**：第1空 preserve ✅；第2空 education ❌（平台答案 training）；第3空 training ❌（平台答案 operate）。
- **全文中文**：机器人手术使整个手术过程能够被记录下来，有助于保存详细的手术信息。这些记录因此成为宝贵的培训工具，让学生和专业人士有机会从真实手术中学习。机器人手术也使医生能够远程进行手术，这种技术称为远程外科手术。

### 逐句结构、错因与语法

1. **Robotic surgery allows the entire procedure to be recorded, helping to preserve detailed surgical information.** 主语 Robotic surgery；谓语 allows；宾语 the entire procedure；宾补 to be recorded 是不定式被动（to be + 过去分词）。helping to preserve 表示伴随结果／作用，help (to) do sth.；preserve information 是保存信息。
2. **This creates a valuable tool for training, giving students and professionals the chance to learn from real operations.** This 指前文手术过程的录制／保存。a valuable tool for training＝宝贵的培训工具。giving... 表示由此带来的结果；give sb. the chance to do sth.＝给某人做某事的机会。training 更强调通过真实手术案例进行技能训练；education 是广义教育，单独使用并非语法错误，但本题上下文更指实践培训。
3. **It also enables doctors to operate remotely, a technique known as telesurgery.** enable + sb. + to + 动词原形；operate 是手术（动词）而 training 是名词/动名词，无法直接表达此处 to operate 的动作。remotely 是副词，修饰 operate。a technique known as telesurgery 是同位语，known as... 为过去分词后置修饰（相当于 a technique that is known as...）。

## 2026-11-19｜FIB D&D #141 Plagiarism｜submitted / committed

- **来源**：用户提交的 FIB D&D #141 Plagiarism 做题结果截图；本次平台成绩 **4/5**，第4空错，其他4空正确。是否首次作答未确认；复测尚未完成。
- **完整正确原文（按截图）**：How is plagiarism detected? It is usually easy for lecturers to **identify** plagiarism within students' work. The University also actively investigates plagiarism in students' assessed work through electronic detection software called Turnitin. This software **compares** students' work **against** text on the Internet, in journal articles and within previously **submitted** work (from LSBU and other institutions) and highlights any matches it **finds**.
- **逐空真实作答**：① identify ✅；② compares ✅；③ against ✅；④ **committed ❌ → 正确 submitted**；⑤ finds ✅。
- **中文释义**：如何检测抄袭？讲师通常容易发现学生作业中的抄袭。学校还会利用名为 Turnitin 的电子检测软件，主动调查评估作业中的抄袭情况。该软件会将学生的作业与互联网文本、期刊文章以及来自本校和其他机构的此前已提交作业进行比较，并突出显示找到的任何匹配内容。
- **错因与语境**：“previously submitted work”＝此前提交过的作业，前面的 previously 修饰过去分词 submitted，后置于名词前表示已完成的动作。committed 常与 **commit plagiarism / commit a crime** 等搭配，表示实施抄袭或犯罪；**commit work** 不是此处所需的“提交作业”含义。不能因文章主题是 plagiarism 就选 committed。
- **语法**：previously（副词）+ submitted（过去分词）+ work（名词）＝以前提交的作业；**compare A against B**＝把A与B对照检查；**compare A with B** 也可以表示比较，不排斥，但本题平台原文用 against。**any matches it finds** 其中 it finds 是省略关系代词 that 的定语从句，修饰 matches。**is usually easy for lecturers to identify** 是 it is + adj. + for sb. + to do 的句型。

## 2026-11-19｜FIB-R #80 The Role of a Union（工会的作用）

- **截图本次得分：3/4**。本次4个空，正确答案依次为 **members / solve problems / conditions / security**。第1、2、4空正确；第3空用户填写 **pressure ❌**，平台正确答案 **conditions ✅**。是否首次作答：未确认；迁移复测尚未作答。
- **完整正确原文（按截图）**：A union is an organization that represents the interests of its **members**. It helps to **solve problems** between employees and employers, and negotiates on behalf of workers for better pay and working **conditions**. Being part of a union can also give workers more confidence and a sense of **security** in the workplace.
- **全文翻译**：工会是代表会员利益的组织。它帮助解决雇员和雇主之间的问题，并代表工人谈判，争取更好的工资与工作条件。加入工会也可以使工人在职场中更有信心和安全感。
- **错因与上下文**：**working conditions** 是“工作条件／工作环境”的固定搭配。前面 **better pay and working conditions** 是工会争取改善工资和工作条件的一组并列待遇。用户误选 pressure，可能想到“工作压力”，但 **better working pressure** 在此不自然，且不能准确表达争取更好工作待遇的意思。说工作压力常用 **work pressure** 或 **pressure at work**，不能把 working pressure 记作本题正确搭配。
- **句子结构**：① “that represents the interests of its members”为定语从句，修饰an organization。② help (to) do sth.，后接 solve problems；“between employees and employers”说明问题发生于劳资双方之间。③ **on behalf of sb.** 介词短语作状语，意为代表某人；**negotiate for sth.** 为争取某事进行谈判；“better pay and working conditions”并列宾语。④ **Being part of a union** 动名词短语作主语；**give sb. sth.** 给某人某物；**a sense of security**＝安全感。
