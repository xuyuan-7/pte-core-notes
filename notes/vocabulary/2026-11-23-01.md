## FIB-RW｜Born to Sing（音乐剧评论）｜本次 0/5

- **截图可见原题场景：**音乐剧 Born to Sing 的评论：演员表演、剧院排队问题，以及最终是否推荐。截图可见全部五个空格的作答与平台答案；题号未显示。
- **空① 原句：**I was \_\_\_ entertained by its fabulous storyline, memorable songs, and big dance numbers.
  - 本次误选：strongly → 平台答案：**thoroughly**。
  - **thoroughly entertained**＝看得非常尽兴；注意 thoroughly enjoyed / thoroughly impressed；对比 strongly recommend / strongly believe（不同动词的程度副词搭配）。
- **空② 原句：**\_\_\_ in London in the 1990s, the musical follows the journeys of four female singers...
  - 本次误选：Setting → 平台答案：**Set**。
  - **be set in + 地点／年代**＝以……为背景；句首 Set in... 是过去分词短语，等于 The musical is set in...。set–set–set。
- **空③ 原句：**The actors in the four main roles put on spectacular performances. In fact, this \_\_\_ to every member of the cast.
  - 本次误选：referred → 平台答案：**applied**。
  - **apply to**＝适用于；this applied to every member＝前面“表演精彩”的评价也适用于所有演员；**refer to**＝指的是／提到，不能只看 to 就选。
- **空④ 原句：**If I could \_\_\_ with anything, it would be with the theater itself.
  - 本次误选：criticize → 平台答案：**find fault**。
  - **find fault with sth.**＝挑……的毛病；criticize 通常直接接宾语（criticize sth.），不说 criticize with sth.。后面的 with 是决定性搭配线索。
- **空⑤ 原句：**The line for the refreshments in the intermission was very long, and I almost missed the start of the second act. \_\_\_, if you're looking for an enjoyable night out, I would recommend getting tickets...
  - 本次误选：Additionally → 平台答案：**Nonetheless**。
  - **nonetheless**＝尽管如此（前句抱怨、后句仍推荐，转折）；additionally＝此外（补充）。
- **错因归类：**搭配与介词（①③④）、过去分词结构（②）、段落转折（⑤）。**复测状态：**未复测；整题红色（0/5）。用户反馈更接近“文章能看懂，但选项之间不知道怎么区分”（A）。

**后续原题复测（11/27）：3/5（60%）**。Set、find fault、Nonetheless 已在原题中纠正；thoroughly 和 applied 两次同空重复错误，知识点🔴，整题本次🟠。保留11/23首次0/5；陌生语境迁移仍未完成。详见 [2026-11-27｜FIB今日词汇与搭配总记录](./2026-11-27-01.md)。

## FIB #1190｜Elizabeth（肖像画）｜本次 2/3

- **原题截图可见片段：**Queen Elizabeth's portraits conveyed the regal image of a powerful monarch—the steadfast, ageless force \_\_\_ England.
- **第一空（错误）：**本次选 **across**；平台答案 **behind**。正确表达：**the steadfast, ageless force behind England**＝英格兰背后坚定而持久的支撑力量。这里 behind 表示“在背后支持／推动”，不是地理位置的后面；across 表示“横穿／遍及”，不能表达这里的支撑关系。
- **第二空（正确）：**a bold reminder **to** guests that he was the man closest to the Queen. **a reminder to sb. that ...**＝向某人提醒／表明……。
- **第三空（正确）：**Although Elizabethan artists drew inspiration from the European Renaissance, **it** was Elizabeth herself who was the national preoccupation. **It was ... who ...**＝强调句结构“正是……”。用户选 it，正确。
- **本次错因：**介词含义及词汇搭配辨析（behind / across）；不是时态错误。整题 2/3，错点待迁移复测，**不标为已经掌握**。
- **复测：**尚未答题，未完成。
