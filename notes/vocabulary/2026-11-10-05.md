## FIB-RW #51｜Music Classes（2026-11-10）

截图结果：**3/4（75%）**。第1空用户 **offers** ❌，正确 **offer**；后3空 related / exposed / understand ✅。

### 原题及答案

Many schools **offer** music classes, just like art classes and physical education classes. Music helps other subjects. The school still retains music classes because research shows that learning music can help improve mathematics and other subjects. School recognized the importance of music **related** alongside maths and physics; studies show that children who are **exposed** to music can better **understand** some concepts in science.

### 用户真实错因

用户明确说：**「本来觉得应该是 offer，我又给改成 offers 了。」** 不是不认识 offer，而是在复核时对主谓一致产生犹豫。

### 知识点

- **Many schools** = many + 复数名词，复数主语 → **offer**，不用 offers。
- **A school / The school** = 单数主语 → **offers**。
- 一般现在时，第三人称单数（he/she/it、单数名词）动词加 -s/-es；复数主语用原形。
- 易对比：**Many schools offer music classes. / This school offers music classes.**
- **be exposed to music** = 接触音乐；**understand concepts in science** = 理解科学概念；**research shows that** = 研究表明。
- 第2空 related 的原句表达不够自然，按平台截图原样保留，不把整句当作标准范例。

### 复习建议

以后检查答案时，先定位整个主语 **Many schools**，再确认动词形式；不因后文出现单数的 **The school** 而回改第一空。完成新语境迁移测试后再判断掌握；不修改平台初次得分。

### 主谓一致延伸测试｜2026-11-10

- **Each of the students \_\_\_ access**：用户选 A have，正确 B has，❌。Each of + 复数名词，核心主语 each 为单数。
- **One of the teachers \_\_\_ responsible**：用户选 B is，✅。One of + 复数名词，主句谓语用单数。
- **One of the students who \_\_\_ interested in science is my friend**：用户选 B are，✅。关系从句 who 指向复数先行词 students，所以从句用 are；主句主语 one 为单数，主句用 is。注意 **the only one of the students who is ...** 中 who 通常指唯一那个人，与此不同。
