## ⭐ 易错辨析

| 英文 | 中文／用法 |
| --- | --- |
| ⭐ platforms / systems | platform侧重开展教学、交流等活动的平台或渠道；system侧重管理、技术或流程系统。两者并非语法互斥，原句与teach via及face-to-face sessions并列时，online platforms更自然。 |
| ⭐ benefit sb. / benefit from sth. | A benefits B＝A使B受益；B benefits from A＝B从A中受益。 |
| ⭐ reduce / decrease | reduce 常表示采取措施主动减少；decrease 可作及物或不及物动词，表示减少或下降。两者有重叠，decrease weekday trips 并非语法错误，但气候政策语境中 reduce 更符合平台答案及常见搭配。 |

> 🔴 FIB-RW #55 Government-funded courses（2/3）：前两空 benefit、flexible 正确；第三空误选 systems，正确为 platforms。原句：Government-funded courses benefit learners by offering training in a flexible location, where instructors teach via both online platforms and face-to-face sessions. 复习重点：online platforms 与 online systems 的语义差异；via 表示“通过某渠道”。

> 🔴 FIB-RW #56 Climate Plan（2/3）：第一空 travel、第三空 public 正确；第二空误选 decrease，平台答案 reduce。原句：A climate plan outlines several strategies to travel around the city, reduce weekday trips, and encourage more use of public transportation and cycling. 核心辨析：reduce/decrease 都能作及物动词，但政策主动减少出行更常用 reduce；不把 decrease 标成语法错误。重点积累 reduce weekday trips、outline strategies、public transportation。

## FIB-RW #63 Water Damage（3/3，全对但第二空犹豫）

原句：When it comes to preventing water damage at home, knowing about shutting down the water supply is very important. This is especially necessary because your pipes might leak. Depending on where the leak happens, you need to turn off the correct valve, since leaks could occur in many different places.

### 生词与辨析

| 英文 | 中文、用法与例句 |
| --- | --- |
| ⭐ necessary /ˈnesəseri/ | 必要的。Taking necessary precautions can prevent damage. |
| critical /ˈkrɪtɪkəl/ | 关键的、至关重要的。This is a critical decision. |
| vital /ˈvaɪtəl/ | 极其重要的、必不可少的。Water plays a vital role in life. |
| essential /ɪˈsenʃəl/ | 必需的、不可缺少的。Communication is an essential skill. |
| valve /vælv/ | 阀门。Turn off the correct valve. |
| leak /liːk/ | v./n. 漏水、泄漏。The pipe might leak. |

### 固定搭配

| 英文 | 中文及例句 |
| --- | --- |
| ⭐ when it comes to + 名词/V-ing | 谈到……时；When it comes to preventing damage, preparation matters. |
| ⭐ shut down the water supply | 关闭供水。 |
| ⭐ turn off the valve | 关掉阀门。 |
| ⭐ depending on | 根据……而定。Depending on the weather, we may stay home. |
| ⭐ play a vital role in | 在……中发挥至关重要的作用。 |
| necessary precautions / necessary steps | 必要的预防措施／步骤。 |
| critical issue / critical decision | 关键问题／决定。 |
| essential skills | 必备技能。 |

### 易错辨析

🟠 第二空在 necessary、critical、vital、essential 之间犹豫，最终选对 necessary。四词都能在原句中构成合语法且大体合理的表达，平台以 necessary 为正确答案；不要死记其他选项绝对错误。necessary强调有必要；critical强调关键性；vital强调极其重要；essential强调必不可少。复习时结合语境、常见搭配辨别。

## FIB-RW #64 Dress Code（4/4，全对）

原文：Employees should wear comfortable shoes, cannot wear slippers, and cannot bring valuable property like necklaces. No special protection will be provided.

### 生词与固定搭配

| 英文 | 中文、音标或例句 |
| --- | --- |
| ⭐ protection /prəˈtekʃən/ | n. 保护、保障。Environmental protection is important. |
| protect /prəˈtekt/ | v. 保护。Protect children from harm. |
| slippers /ˈslɪpərz/ | n. 拖鞋（复数）。 |
| necklaces /ˈnekləsɪz/ | n. 项链（复数）。 |
| ⭐ provide protection | 提供保护。The company provides protection. |
| special protection | 特殊保护。 |
| environmental protection | 环境保护。 |
| protection against damage | 防止损坏的保护措施。 |
| ⭐ protect sb. from sth. | 保护某人免受……伤害。 |
| valuable property | 贵重物品、财物。 |

### 重点语法：一般将来时被动语态

⭐ **will be + 过去分词**：表示将被……。主动：The company will provide special protection. 被动：Special protection will be provided. 原句：\*\*No special protection will be provided.\*\*＝不会提供特殊保护措施。No + 名词可直接表达否定，也可改写为 Special protection will not be provided。

🟢 本题四空 shoes、slippers、necklaces、protection 全对；用户特别要求理解并记录最后一句。结合上下文，指贵重物品不会得到特殊保护，并不必然表示企业完全没有责任。
