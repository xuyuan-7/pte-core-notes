## FIB-R #74｜Flexibility（2026-11-10）

**本次重做：3/5（60%），用户选择 E B D C A；正确 E D B C A。**

### 原题（挖空版）

Flexibility is a vital (1) \_\_\_\_\_ of physical fitness. Maintaining flexible (2) \_\_\_\_\_ is essential for performing our daily (3) \_\_\_\_\_ without pain or restriction. A (4) \_\_\_\_\_ of 10 to 15 minutes of simple stretching exercises each day can significantly contribute to your overall wellbeing. These exercises not only keep the body healthy but also provide more (5) \_\_\_\_\_ to engage in the things you enjoy doing, ensuring you stay active as you age.

选项：A opportunities；B activities；C minimum；D joints；E component。

### 逐空作答

1 component ✅；2 activities → joints ❌；3 joints → activities ❌；4 minimum ✅；5 opportunities ✅。

### 重点辨析与复习

- **flexible joints**＝灵活的关节；joint（名词）关节。此题身体柔韧性、无疼痛语境对应关节。
- **perform daily activities**＝进行日常活动；activity 复数 activities。
- **a vital component of**＝……的重要组成部分；**a minimum of 10 to 15 minutes**＝至少10到15分钟；**contribute to overall wellbeing**＝有助于整体身心健康；**opportunities to engage in**＝参与……的机会。
- 暂定错因：第2、3空词义/搭配互换；用户尚未确认是词汇不认识、语境误判还是猜测。请复测 flexible joints 与 daily activities。

## FIB-R #75｜Cycling Club（2026-11-10）

**本次重做：0/3（0%），用户选择 E B D；正确 C D A。**

### 原题（挖空版）

The cycling club offers a range of guided rides and workshops designed for both adults and (1) \_\_\_\_\_ teenagers. Whether you are complete amateurs looking to gain confidence on two wheels or experienced riders seeking adventure, every bicycle (2) \_\_\_\_\_ is supervised by a certified (3) \_\_\_\_\_. Participants are provided with expert guidance to navigate scenic trails safely, ensuring an enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

选项：A instructor；B voyage；C mature；D outing；E amateurs。

### 逐空作答

1 amateurs → mature ❌；2 voyage → outing ❌；3 outing → instructor ❌。

### 重点辨析与复习

- **mature teenagers**＝较成熟的青少年；mature 是形容词；amateurs 是复数名词（amateur 单数也可作形容词，故主要问题是所给 amateurs 的形式及含义不符）。
- **bicycle outing**＝骑自行车郊游/出行；outing 通常指短途休闲活动；voyage 通常指较长航程（尤指海上/太空），不适合此处的自行车活动。
- **certified instructor**＝持证教练/指导员；be supervised by a certified instructor＝由持证教练监督指导。
- **complete amateurs**＝完全的新手；**gain confidence**＝建立信心；**on two wheels**＝骑自行车；**scenic trails**＝风景优美的小径；**provide sb. with expert guidance**＝为某人提供专业指导。
- 暂定错因：第1空词形与语义、第2空 outing/voyage 的场景差别、第3空角色与语境；实际个人错因待确认。
