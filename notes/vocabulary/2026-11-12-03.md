## 2026-11-12｜FIB #1263｜BC Driver Insurance

### 截图可见原文

New B.C. residents will only be required to provide their driver licence history — proof of insurance is no longer required. However, their premiums will be adjusted for the first three years **of** driving in B.C. **due to** the increased risks associated with driving in a new area. Each year you remain crash-free, your discount increases. ICBC **will credit** you with up to 15 years of driving experience (up from eight years) upon receipt of a driver's abstract **showing** the original date that you received your licence. When obtaining your B.C. licence, you can provide your original driver's abstract in person at a driver licensing ... \[截图在此处截断\]

### 理解与搭配

| English | 中文／用法 |
| --- | --- |
| due to + 名词短语 | 由于……；due to the increased risks |
| because + 完整从句 | 因为……；because the risks are higher |
| because of + 名词短语 | 由于……；because of the increased risks |
| insurance premiums | 保费；premium 为名词 |
| be associated with | 与……相关 |
| remain crash-free | 保持无事故状态 |
| credit sb. with sth. | 认可某人具备……经验等 |
| up to | 最多 |
| upon receipt of | 收到……之后 |
| driver's abstract | 驾驶记录摘要/证明 |
| be required to do | 被要求做…… |
| proof of insurance | 保险证明 |
| no longer | 不再 |
| showing the original date | 显示最初日期；现在分词后置定语，等于 that shows... |

### 四空判断

1. first three years **of** driving：驾驶的头三年。
2. **due to** the increased risks：后面是名词短语；because 不可直接接名词短语。
3. ICBC **will credit** you with：未来将认可某人拥有一定年限的驾驶经验。
4. driver's abstract **showing** the date：记录主动显示日期，用现在分词后置修饰；不是被显示。

### 迁移测试反馈

- A: The outdoor event was cancelled \_\_\_ severe weather conditions. **due to** 或 **because of** 都成立。
- B: The outdoor event was cancelled \_\_\_ the weather conditions were severe. **because** 正确。
- 用户回答 A=3、B=1，均正确；A=2 也正确。此测试发生在讲解之后，尚不能证明独立稳定掌握，维持橙色。

**复习方式：** 日后混合 due to / because / because of 与主动、被动分词的真实语境，不重复原句机械练习。
