### 原题与用户答案（截图保留）

Contexts is a quarterly magazine of the American Sociological Association about society and social behavior. Directed to **anyone** interested in the latest sociological ideas and research, Contexts seeks to apply new knowledge, stimulate fresh thinking, and disseminate important information **produced** by the discipline. The publication's articles synthesize key findings, weave **together** diverse strands of work, draw out implications for policy, and debate issues of controversy. By design, it is not a technical journal, **but** a magazine for diverse readers, **those** who wish to be current about social science knowledge, emerging trends, and their relevance.

### 搭配与语法复习

- **directed to anyone interested in...** 面向任何对……感兴趣的人；anyone 为不定代词。
- **information produced by the discipline** 该学科产生的信息；produced 是过去分词作后置定语，表示被产生。
- **weave together diverse strands of work** 把不同研究线索整合起来。
- **not A, but B** 不是A，而是B。
- **those who...** 那些……的人；those 后可接 who 定语从句。
- **disseminate information** 传播信息；**synthesize findings** 综合研究发现。

- 第1空：<code>Directed to anyone interested in ...</code>，anyone 指任何对社会学观点和研究感兴趣的人；<code>anyone interested in...</code> 相当于 <code>anyone who is interested in...</code>；此处 directed to 后需要一个名词性对象。
