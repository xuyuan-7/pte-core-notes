## FIB-R #35｜Sports（2026-11-12）

**本次结果：** 3/3 全对；是否靠理解或记忆尚未核实，不擅自标绿。

**原题完整句：**<br />Sports were **tightly** linked to survival skills, but now they are primarily for **recreational** purposes and enjoyment. Activities such as swimming, hunting, and fishing were **originally** for obtaining food, but have now become competitive sports.

### 固定搭配／表达（英文｜中文）

| English | 中文释义及用法 |
| --- | --- |
| be tightly linked to sth. | 与某事紧密相关；be linked to = 与……有关联，tightly 强调程度 |
| survival skills | 生存技能 |
| be primarily for sth. | 主要用于某种目的；primarily = mainly |
| for recreational purposes | 为了娱乐／消遣目的；for ... purposes 常见搭配 |
| for enjoyment | 为了享受、娱乐 |
| obtain food / obtaining food | 获取食物；for 后接名词或动名词，故 for obtaining food |
| competitive sports | 竞技体育、竞技运动 |

### 单词（英文｜中文）

| English | 中文释义及注意点 |
| --- | --- |
| tightly /ˈtaɪtli/ | 紧密地、牢固地；副词修饰 linked |
| recreational /ˌrekriˈeɪʃənəl/ | 娱乐性的、消遣的；形容词修饰 purposes |
| originally /əˈrɪdʒənəli/ | 起初、原本；副词，和 now 对照 |
| survival /səˈvaɪvəl/ | 生存；survival skills |
| primarily /praɪˈmerəli/ | 主要地、首先 |
| obtain /əbˈteɪn/ | 获得、取得；obtain food |
| competitive /kəmˈpetətɪv/ | 竞争性的、竞技性的；competitive sports |

**上下文逻辑：** but now ... / but have now become ... 均形成“过去 vs 现在”的对照；因此 originally 对应过去，recreational 对应当代休闲用途。题目正确答案 tightly、recreational、originally；不保留错误选项作为复习答案。
